Souvla, the Cypriot way.

Three things, in this order. The machine does the rest.

Light it

Charcoal only. Let it burn down to a grey-orange glow before anything goes near it. The hood holds the heat, so you use less than you think.

Thread it

Lamb shoulder or neck in fist-sized pieces. Salt, oregano, lemon, nothing clever. Long skewers for the big pieces, short ones for souvlakia.

Turn it

Set the speed controller, close the hood for the first hour, open it for the crust. Baste with the drippings and don't rush it.
