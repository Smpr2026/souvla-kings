# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Greek- and Cypriot-Australian families and backyard cooks across Australia, plus church fetes, clubs and small caterers. They arrive from Instagram, Facebook or TikTok, usually on a phone, after seeing a video of lamb turning. They want to know what they get, what it costs delivered, and how to order.

## Product Purpose
Souvla Kings hand-builds 100% stainless steel Cypriot souvla rotisseries (foukou) in Sydney: hooded, motorised, speed-controlled, delivered free Australia-wide. Orders are taken by phone or message on 0429 543 435. The site's single job is to make people ring or message.

## Brand Personality
Heavy, hand-welded, festive. Voice: plain-spoken, proud, a bit of swagger ("Nothing will ever compare to an original Cypriot souvla", their own line). Emotional goal: the smell of charcoal and the sound of the motor, a Saturday in the backyard with everyone over.

## Anti-references
- Generic "dark BBQ site": black background, flame stock photo, orange gradient buttons.
- Greek-flag blue and white, meander borders, Greek-letter display fonts.
- SaaS landing scaffolding: eyebrow labels, three-icon feature cards, hero metrics.

## Design Principles
- Show the machine turning: the product is motion, so the hero is the product working, not a photo of it.
- Everything is stainless and charcoal: light comes from the coals, surfaces are steel, text is steel-white.
- Prices are the headline: they market by "$749 Delivered", so the price is the product name.
- One phone number, everywhere: every section ends at the number.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Reduced-motion users get a still scene and static ribbons. Body text at 4.5:1 or better on the olive-charcoal ground. All controls keyboard-reachable with visible focus. No information carried by animation alone.
